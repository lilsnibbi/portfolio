## A bot with a purpose. And a personality.

bamu is the custom Discord bot behind my community. It brings moderation, support tickets, and everyday automation together in one place, with a little personality along the way.

The project lives at the intersection of two things I enjoy: building backend software and bringing people together.

## Built around the community.

A community has a lot of small, recurring needs. bamu brings those pieces into a single, modular TypeScript and Bun codebase.

- **Moderation.** Tools to help look after the server and the people in it.
- **Support tickets.** A dedicated place for members to ask for help.
- **Automation.** Taking care of repetitive community tasks.

> **The idea**
>
> Useful tools should make the community easier to run and a nicer place to be.

## Under the hood.

bamu is built with TypeScript and runs on Bun. Its modular structure brings the different community features together without putting every responsibility in the same place.

It's a closed-source project, built specifically for my community. The public place to find out more is the community itself.

## The human side of the project.

Running a community of more than 2000 people gives this project a very real reason to exist. It's software for a place where people game, build things, and hang out.

That makes bamu a particularly personal project: a tool for the community I'm part of, with room to keep improving alongside it.
